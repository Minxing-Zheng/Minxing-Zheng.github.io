I am a Ph.D. student at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by [Prof. Shixiang Zhu](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/shixianz/). My research focuses on uncertainty quantification, particularly conformal prediction, and human-AI collaboration for decision-making.

I completed my undergraduate studies in Statistics with a double major in Applied Mathematics at Renmin University of China. I earned a master’s degree in Statistics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison, where I was advised by [Prof. Kris Sankaran](https://krisrs1128.github.io/LSLab/_includes/team) and [Prof. Keith Levin](https://stat.wisc.edu/staff/levin-keith/), contributing to research on machine learning interpretability and statistical network analysis. I then earned a master’s degree in Statistics at the University of Southern California, where I worked with [Prof. Paromita Dubey](https://www.paromitadubey.com/) on statistical change point detection, before beginning my Ph.D. at Carnegie Mellon.
