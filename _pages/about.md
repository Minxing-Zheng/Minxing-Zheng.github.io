<section class="home-section research-overview" aria-labelledby="research-heading">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="section-kicker">Research</p>
    <h2 id="research-heading">Statistical foundations for AI you can trust.</h2>
    <p>My work connects rigorous uncertainty guarantees with real decision problems—from scientific systems to secure machine learning.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="focus-grid">
    <article class="focus-card">
      <span class="focus-number">01</span>
      <h3>Uncertainty quantification</h3>
      <p>Flexible prediction and uncertainty sets with finite-sample validity, especially for complex and structured outputs.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="focus-card">
      <span class="focus-number">02</span>
      <h3>Conformal inference</h3>
      <p>Conformal methods for generative models and latent quantities that are not directly observed during calibration.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="focus-card">
      <span class="focus-number">03</span>
      <h3>Reliable &amp; scientific AI</h3>
      <p>Statistical learning for safety monitoring, robust decisions, model security, and human–AI collaboration.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
